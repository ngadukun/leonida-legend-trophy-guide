    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five phases, in the order that wastes the least time. Nothing in the list needs a second playthrough if you follow the two habits in Phase 0.

    Phase 0 — Two habits that save a replay

    • Keep a manual save before big story decisions. Actions Speak Louder is a choice trophy (“choices made, for better or worse”); a save from before the decision is the difference between five minutes and a second campaign.
    • Never reload away a failure. Bad Luck, I Guess (“second time's the charm”) is earned by failing once and succeeding on the retry — take the retry the game offers.

    Phase 1 — The story, chapters 1 to the finale

    Thirteen trophies are pure campaign: the four chapter trophies (Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Vice City, Baby!, Top Of The World, Ma), the named missions (Just Like Paradise, Trust, Break the Bank, Family Matters, DWNFALL, Hi-Jacked, Snow Storm) and the finale itself, You Gotta Know When to Get Off. Play them in order; none can be skipped. From Chapter 3 onwards the map is fully open, so start the map-wide grinds in parallel rather than saving them all for the end.

    Phase 2 — Every strand, before the finale

    Five characters have strands that end in a named final mission: Andy (Road to Rehabilitation), Kenton (This Was No Boat Accident), Billy (The Lone Rider), Randy (Supply & Demand) and Tyrone (Spin On This!). Treat the finale as a point of no return and close all five strands first; then finish eight stranger strands anywhere in Leonida for Strangers & Freaks, and run Bret's hunting strand for Fair Game.

    Phase 3 — The open-world grind

    The long tail, all doable in free roam: 250 stunts (Cunning Stunts), every Shooting Range gold (The Shootist), a drone race win at every location (Top Fun), 20 different race wins (Earn Cash, Not Kudos), Cal's fishing challenge (Baited My Tackle), one arcade game (Stick Fiddler), one attempt at every activity type (Master of None), 50 challenges (One Last Thing), every meditation spot (Positive Mental Attitude), 100 WAiNK It spots (You WAiNKer!), 3 card sets (Don't Touch My Deck), 100 vehicles discovered (Whip It Good) and the whole map uncovered (The Goodtime State). Sweep region by region and these mostly complete each other.

    Phase 4 — Crime, partners and money

    The set-piece feats: a silent jewelry robbery (Sneakthief), five bank vaults (Maximum Withdrawal), an armored truck (A Classic Blitz Play), one illegal operation per gang (Pest Control) and $250,000 of laundered Marked Bills (Dime on the Dollar). With your partner: escape a 4-star wanted level together (Hot Together), rob three shops back-to-back without the heat clearing (Retail Therapy) and the cryptic Personal Best. In combat: five takedowns in one Combat Focus (Gunslinger), time in a Rhino tank (Challenging Tank) and a melee drive-by set (Ten Pin). Then the money sinks: $100,000 spent (Treat Yo Self), every garage bought (Little Boxes) and 10 vehicles registered (On the Spot). Keep an eye out the whole game for the oddity trophies — an unscheduled departure at VCIA (Catch Me If You Can), a cocktail on Ocean Beach (In The Beginning…), a showroom exit (Capital Offense), a bowling game (Let's Go Bowling) and whatever The Truth Is Out There turns out to be.

    Phase 5 — 100% and the platinum

    A Long Road asks for 100% on the game's own completion counter; by now it is a mop-up of whatever category is short. When the 54th trophy pops, Vice Got a Price! — the platinum — follows on its own.

    Watch-outs

    The trophies that can go wrong: strand finales to finish before the story's end, the choice to save before, the failure not to reload away, one clean-run robbery, and the cryptic descriptions whose guides are honest about being the plainest reading of the official text.
